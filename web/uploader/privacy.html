Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Overview

The openFrameworks Uploader ("the App") is a browser-based tool that helps you publish an openFrameworks sketch to your own GitHub account. It runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate a server that stores your data, and we do not sell, rent, or share any personal information. This policy explains what the App touches and why.

What we collect

The App itself collects nothing on a server of ours. Specifically:

  • GitHub access token — the token you paste is held only in your browser tab (sessionStorage) for the length of your session. It is sent directly to GitHub to create your repository and is never transmitted to us. Closing the tab or signing out erases it.
  • Your source files — the files you drop in are read in your browser and uploaded directly to your GitHub repository. They do not pass through any server we control.
  • No analytics, cookies, or tracking — the App sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics or trackers.

Third-party services

To do its job, the App talks directly to GitHub (the GitHub API and GitHub Pages) using the token you provide. Your use of GitHub is governed by GitHub's own Privacy Statement and Terms of Service. Anything you publish becomes a public repository and public web page on GitHub, subject to their policies.

Data you publish is public

By design, the App creates a public repository and a public GitHub Pages site. Do not upload secrets, credentials, personal data, or anything you do not intend to make public.

Security

Your token is stored only in the current browser tab and is used solely for direct, encrypted (HTTPS) requests to GitHub. You can revoke the token at any time from your GitHub token settings. We recommend giving tokens a short expiration.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for students and educators in a course setting. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project maintainers, Max Fishman and Luisa Pinzon (Executive Directors — Digital Arts).
